Fernando Sor’s Op. 60, Introduction à l’étude de la guitare, collects 25 progressive lessons, from the simplest to those with several voices.

The tablature was made by algorithms. Although we review it manually, you may find some errors. In the Mutopia lessons, the numbers above the notes are the left-hand fingers from the source edition, and the tab respects them.

The notes come from the LilyPond editions of the Mutopia Project (Fabrice De Volder and Hajo Dezelski, Creative Commons BY-SA license).

The lessons Mutopia does not publish (14 onwards) are transcribed by hand from the public-domain scans on IMSLP (Gitarrefreund edition, Munich, 1915-17), checked against the original Paris edition. Those pieces are in the public domain and carry no left-hand fingering.
