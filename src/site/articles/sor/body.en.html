Fernando Sor’s Op. 60, Introduction à l’étude de la guitare, collects 25 progressive lessons, from the simplest to those with several voices.

The tablature was made by algorithms. Although we review it manually, you may find some errors. In the Mutopia lessons, the numbers above the notes are the left-hand fingers from the source edition, and the tab respects them.

Lessons 1–13 come from the LilyPond editions in the Mutopia Project (Fabrice De Volder and Hajo Dezelski), under the Creative Commons BY-SA license identified in each PDF. Lessons 14–15 were transcribed from Gitarrefreund (Munich, 1915–17); from lesson 16 onwards the source is the Paris print, chez l’auteur (c. 1837), checked against other historical editions. These public-domain prints are available on IMSLP. The new transcriptions add no left-hand fingering; their tablature uses the same engine as the rest of the site.

Lesson 25 tunes the sixth string to D. In its natural harmonics, diamond noteheads show the resulting sound and the TAB shows the contact fret. Sor’s special 3 means touching slightly beyond the third fret towards the bridge (approximately 3.16), without pressing the string down.
