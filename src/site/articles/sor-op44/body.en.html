Fernando Sor’s 24 petites pièces progressives, Op. 44, are intended for beginners on the guitar. The collection starts with a simple line and gradually introduces sustained bass notes, several voices and new keys.

Open the available scores, browse them in the viewer and download their PDFs with tablature. The TAB is generated by algorithms and checked manually; errors may still remain.

Each PDF identifies its source and credits. Historical Paris editions published by Sor and Pacini are available at IMSLP. This collection has its own numbering and is separate from the Op. 60 lessons.
