Matteo Carcassi (1792–1853) wrote sixteen preludes in progressive keys for his Méthode complète pour Guitare, Op. 59. Each card shows the first two systems; a click downloads the whole prelude as a PDF, with the tab staff below the notation.

The tab fingering is a suggestion: most notes can be played in more than one place on the neck. The numbers above the notes are the left-hand fingers of the edition.
