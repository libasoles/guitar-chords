Matteo Carcassi’s Allegretto in C alternates two chords: C and G7. Below are the sheet music and the tab for measures 1 to 4 (the first one is the pickup); measures 3 and 4 repeat in a loop. Click the example to download the full score as a PDF.

Full form and decomposed form

The diagrams show C and G7 as they appear in the site’s chord database, in their standard open position: that is the full form, with all of its notes. Carcassi’s arpeggio does not play them all. Taking only some notes of a chord is decomposition, and what is left is the decomposed form.

%%FORMS_ALLEGRETTO%%

In G7, strings 4 and 5 are left out: the bass stays on string 6, with three high notes. In C, string 4 is left out and the bass is on string 5. The decomposed form needs fewer fingers than the full one.
