The twelve studies of Fernando Sor’s Op. 6 and the twenty-four of his Op. 35, in the order of the edition. Each piece keeps the number it has in its opus, and they are not all here: the ones that could be checked are published.

The notes come from the LilyPond editions of the Mutopia Project (Glen Larsen, Creative Commons BY-SA license; Op. 35 No. 14 is by Jakob Bagterp, in the public domain). Each one is compared with Mutopia’s own MIDI and score before it appears here; the PDF is generated again, with the tab calculated by the engine of the site’s “Sheet Music with Tablature” tool. Being BY-SA, the PDFs and MusicXML files on this page are shared under the same license.

The tab fingering is a suggestion: most notes can be played in more than one place on the fretboard. The numbers above the notes are the left-hand fingers from the source edition, and the tab respects them. Op. 6 No. 7 and No. 9 call for the sixth string tuned down to D (the tab shows it). Each PDF carries a credit to the source edition at the bottom.
